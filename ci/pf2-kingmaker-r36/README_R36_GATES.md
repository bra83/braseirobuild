# Kingmaker R36 official rule gates

Source: Kingmaker.pdf, pages 522, 530 and 531.

- Claim Hex: pay 1 RP before the check; do not charge twice on success. Allow 1/2/3 attempts at kingdom levels 1/4/9.
- Quell Unrest: critical success reduces 1d6; critical failure chooses 1d4 Unrest or two distinct Ruins; prevent reusing the same skill in consecutive turns.
- Collect Taxes: +2/+1 circumstance bonus on Economy checks for the rest of the turn, never RP.

These are implementation gates, not claims of completed Android validation.
